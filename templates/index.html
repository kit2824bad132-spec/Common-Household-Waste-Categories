Household Reference

Common Household Waste Categories

Proper segregation keeps materials out of landfills and accelerates municipal recycling.

🍃

Organic / Biodegradable

Decomposable kitchen and garden waste suitable for home composting or biogas production.

Recommended Bin: Green
  • Fruit & vegetable peels
  • Coffee grounds & tea bags
  • Leftover food scraps
  • Dry leaves and plant cuttings
♻️

Dry Recyclable

Clean, dry materials that can be mechanically sorted and reprocessed into new products.

Recommended Bin: Blue
  • Cardboard boxes & paper
  • Clean plastic bottles & jugs
  • Aluminum cans & foil
  • Glass jars & bottles
⚡

Hazardous & E-Waste

Dangerous substances that require specialized handling to prevent toxic environmental leakage.

Recommended Bin: Red / Designated E-drop
  • Spent household batteries
  • Old phones, chargers & wires
  • Fluorescent bulbs & tubes
  • Chemical cleaners & aerosol cans
🗑️

Residual / Non-Recyclable

Non-biodegradable items contaminated with soil, grease, or composed of mixed polymers.

Recommended Bin: Grey / Black
  • Sanitary napkins & diapers
  • Greasy pizza boxes
  • Multi-layered snack packaging
  • Broken ceramics & mirrors
About The Project

Automating Sustainable Habits with AI

Household waste contamination is the number one cause of recycling program failure. When recyclable items are improperly mixed with wet organic waste or dangerous chemicals, entire batches end up in landfills.

EcoSort is designed as a foundational deep-learning web platform. By enabling households and recycling facilities to take a photo of an item, computer vision classification models can instantly identify the material type and direct it to the correct bin.
